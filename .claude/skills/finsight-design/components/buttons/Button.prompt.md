The one button. Every CTA in the system is this pill; there are no square buttons.

```jsx
<Button size="lg">Get started</Button>
<Button variant="secondary-light">See prices</Button>
<Button variant="outline-on-dark">Talk to sales</Button>
<Button variant="tertiary-text">Learn more</Button>
```

- `primary` is the only coloured action — one or two blue moments per band, no more.
- `size="lg"` (56px) is reserved for the homepage hero; `md` (44px) everywhere else.
- On dark bands use `secondary-dark` or `outline-on-dark` beside the blue primary.
- Never green/red buttons — trading colours are text-only semantics.
